🌸 Our Story

It started in 2019 when our founder Arjun, a lifelong Gundam builder and shonen fan, got burned by a fake Zero Two statue he ordered from an overseas reseller. ₹6,000 wasted on a terrible knockoff.

He spent 6 months building direct relationships with licensed manufacturers in Japan, Bandai distributors, and certified PVC suppliers — then opened AnimeBox with 30 authentic figures.

Today we carry 500+ figures, ship to every Indian state, and employ a team of fellow otaku who inspect every single figure before it leaves our warehouse.

❓ FAQ
Are your figures authentic / licensed? ▼
100% yes. Every figure includes a holographic authenticity sticker. We source exclusively from licensed manufacturers and authorised Bandai/Good Smile distributors. Bootlegs are never stocked.
How is packaging handled? ▼
We use double-boxed shipping with custom foam inserts. The collector box is never exposed. For large statues, we use professional art transport cases with corner bracing.
What is the return policy? ▼
30-day hassle-free returns. If your figure arrives damaged or misrepresented, we'll replace it or give a full refund. We've never refused a valid claim.
Do you do limited edition pre-orders? ▼
Yes! Join our Collector Club on Discord for first access to limited edition pre-orders, including exclusive colourways that sell out within hours.
Are garage kits suitable for beginners? ▼
Garage kits vary — we rate each as beginner, intermediate, or expert. Beginner kits come with a painting guide. We also stock pre-assembled premium resin figures if you prefer display-ready items.
